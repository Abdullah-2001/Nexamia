Nexamia International Limited

An international education, leadership development and educational tourism organisation. We organise conferences, leadership programmes, workshops, training sessions, networking events and educational experiences for students, young professionals and emerging leaders.

What we do

Our activities combine education, leadership development, international exposure, cultural exchange and travel-based learning, connecting participants from different countries. Events run in the UK and internationally with universities, institutions, speakers, trainers and businesses.

Explore common questions

Who are Nexamia experiences for?+

Students, young professionals and emerging leaders curious about the world. No previous international experience is needed. Current proposed programmes are for participants aged 18 and over.

Do I need to be based in the UK?+

No. Nexamia welcomes interest from across the world. Travel arrangements, entry requirements and eligibility depend on the destination and confirmed programme.

How do I take part in a programme?+

Explore upcoming experiences and register your interest on an event page. It is free and does not reserve a place. We will share confirmed details and application information when available.

Are the event dates and fees confirmed?+

The experiences shown are proposed programmes. Dates, venues, speakers, fees and inclusions are subject to confirmation. No payments are being accepted. Please wait for written confirmation before booking travel or accommodation.
